# Limitations

1. The four public industry datasets are independent and require disclosed synthetic cross-domain linkage.
2. The default protocol does not provide cryptographic security.
3. Histogram GBDT sends target-derived gradient/Hessian aggregates and routing signals that can leak information.
4. Synthetic benchmarks simplify institution-level operational heterogeneity.
5. A single-process transport validates protocol structure, not real network behavior or latency.
6. Performance gains are empirical and dataset-specific; negative and inconclusive results must be retained.
